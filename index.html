Our Story

More than a haircut.
It's a ritual.

Sid Barbershop opened in 2001 on Pavia St. in Roxas City with a simple idea: every man deserves to walk out feeling confident. Over the years we've become a neighborhood favorite, a place where friends catch up, stories get told, and the fade is always clean.

Our barbers combine old-school technique with today's trends, using premium products and sterilized tools for a safe, comfortable experience.
